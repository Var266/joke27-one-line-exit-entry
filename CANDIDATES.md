- Mid: 5 (final shush at full volume — good image, slow landing), 6 (going down — predictable pun), 8 (pairs well — neat but thin), 11 (job is the rock — solid, familiar shape).
- Top 4: 1, 2, 3, 4.
- 3 vs 1: 3 is funny but leans on the worn standup-meeting trope (criterion 2 weakens); 1's "cleared for landing" turn is fresher. 1 wins.
